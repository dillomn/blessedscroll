Set up HTTPS on your iPhone

One-time setup. Once it's done, Save goes straight to Photos.

  1. In Safari, download the certificate and tap Allow.
    Download certificate
  2. Open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap BlessedScroll Local CA, then Install.
  3. Open Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn on BlessedScroll Local CA.
  4. Open BlessedScroll over HTTPS and add it to your Home Screen if you like.
    Open BlessedScroll

The certificate can only vouch for this one address (it's limited to this IP), so trusting it doesn't let it impersonate any other site.
